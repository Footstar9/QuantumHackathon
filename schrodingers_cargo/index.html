How to play

A single qubit of precious cargo is spread across 9 physical qubits in three crates (the Shor code). Two players take turns.

  1. Noise (secretly) flips up to budget qubits with X bit flip, Z phase flip, or Y both.
  2. Pass the computer. The Decoder never sees the errors, only the 8 check lamps:
    • orange lamps compare the bits of two neighbouring qubits. They light for X or Y.
    • blue lamps compare the phase of two neighbouring crates. They light for Z or Y.
  3. The Decoder applies fixes with the same switches and presses Ship it.
  4. The cargo is safe if the leftover error (Noise × fix) does nothing to the stored qubit. Decoder scores. Otherwise Noise scores.
  5. Roles swap every round. Highest score at the end wins.

Tip for Decoders: the fewest errors that explain the lamps is usually right, but a sneaky Noise can disguise two errors as one.

Tip for Noise: two flips in the same crate look like one flip on the third qubit…
